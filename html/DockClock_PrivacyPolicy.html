Privacy Policy for DockClock

Last updated: August 27, 2026

DockClock ("the App") does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or usage information whatsoever, with the sole exception of the crash reporting described below. This privacy policy is provided to explain, in full, what that means.

Data Collection

DockClock does not collect any data. The App does not use analytics or advertising services, and it does not otherwise communicate with any server operated by us or any third party.

Crash Reporting

DockClock uses Google Play's crash reporting service. If the App crashes, you will be prompted for permission to send a crash report to Google Play servers. No crash report is sent without your permission. Crash reports do not contain any personal or identifying information, other than the model of the device you were using at the time of the crash.

Permissions

Any device permissions requested by the App (such as access to the clock, display, or network settings) are used solely to provide the App's features on your device and are never used to collect or transmit information.

Third-Party Services

Other than the Google Play crash reporting service described above, DockClock does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children's Privacy

Since DockClock does not collect any data from anyone, it does not knowingly collect any data from children under the age of 13 or any other age.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy ever changes, an updated version will be posted at this same location with a revised "Last updated" date.
